# Engagement comments

Commenting on other people's posts is how an account gets noticed by audiences it does not
reach yet: a useful comment under a popular Reel sits in front of everyone reading the
thread, and anyone curious can open the user's profile from it. The creator reads it too,
and remembers who adds something and who leaves an emoji.

## What Saypage can do here

Nothing directly. Saypage works on the comments under the **user's own** posts. It cannot
open other accounts' posts, and it cannot comment, like or follow for the user, so call no
Saypage tool in this skill. The user shares the post (pasted caption, screenshot, or a
description of the Reel) and posts the comment themselves. Do not post through a browser
for them either: automated engagement is against Instagram's terms and leads to blocks.

## Pick the kind of comment

Choose based on the post in front of you:

| Kind | When | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Add to it** | the user knows a variation, a tip, a detail | "Brown the butter first and it gets nutty, worth a try." |
| **Their result** | the user tried something similar | what happened, in one or two sentences |
| **A real question** | the post left out the tricky bit | one specific question the creator will want to answer |
| **The moment** | one detail stood out | name it: "the fold at 0:14 is so clean" |
| **Kind pushback** | the user genuinely disagrees | what they agree with, then where they differ |
| **One line** | the post needs nothing added | under ten words, and true or funny |

## Rules

- Short: a sentence or two fits under a Reel without being folded away.
- Write something that only makes sense under this post. A comment that would fit under a
  hundred similar posts gets ignored.
- Sell nothing here: no link, no offer, no "come see my page". Creators block that, and
  their audience notices.
- Skip the reflex openers ("Amazing 😍", "Obsessed", "Yes!!") and skip repeating
  what the post just said.
- Match the post's language, and the user's voice (`natural-voice`).
- Timing helps: comments left soon after a Reel goes up are near the top when it spreads.

## The daily round

About ten to fifteen minutes a day, best done before the user publishes their own post.
Keep a list of roughly ten accounts and spread the comments over the week, so no account
sees the user under every single post:

- **Larger accounts** with the audience the user wants: comment soon after they post.
- **Peers** of a similar size in the same field: the ones most likely to reply and return
  the visit.
- **Local and business contacts**: suppliers, neighbouring shops, possible partners or
  clients. Let a relationship build in the comments over several weeks; only then think
  about a DM (`dm-writing`).

## Output

Two options of different kinds per post, labelled, and which one you would post:

```
ON @account's Reel about lamination

[Add to it]
Chilling the dough between every fold saved my croissants last summer, 26 °C kitchen and all.

[A real question]
Do you rest it in the fridge or the freezer between turns? Mine tears on the third fold.

Post the first: it adds something and shows you bake too.
```

For a batch, ask for the five to ten posts in one message and give one comment each.
